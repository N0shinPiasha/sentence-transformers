# SBERT Semantic Similarity Comparison

Here is the proof of execution and the comparison of semantic similarity scores generated by the `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` model:

### Test Sentences
1. "A man is playing the guitar."
2. "A musician is playing a song on his acoustic guitar."
3. "The weather is very sunny in Sydney today."

### Execution Output & Comparison
| Comparison | Cosine Similarity Score | Semantic Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sentence 1 vs 2** | `0.7758` | Highly similar (Both describe a person playing guitar) |
| **Sentence 1 vs 3** | `0.0682` | Not similar (Guitar vs. Weather) |
